Retro Alien Invasion · Privacy

Your game stays on your device.

Retro Alien Invasion works offline. The app does not collect or transmit personal data.

Effective October 5, 2026 · Developer: Jeff Geissler

Using the app

The app does not require an account and has no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Your high score is saved locally on your device using Apple’s UserDefaults. It is not sent to the developer or synced between devices.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, Jeff Geissler receives your email address and whatever message or attachments you send. This information is used to answer your request and troubleshoot problems. To request deletion of support correspondence, email scale.with.jeff@outlook.com.

If you open a public issue on GitHub, the information you post is public and handled under GitHub’s policies. Please do not post private information in an issue.

Visiting these pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies, or contact forms. GitHub may process website access information under its Privacy Statement. Visiting this site is separate from playing the offline app.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change, with a revised effective date.

Questions? Visit the support page.
