How to play on iPhone

Drag your ship, or hold the left and right controls to move. Tap Fire to shoot. Tap Pause to stop or resume the game.

How to play on Mac

Click the game window, then use the left and right arrow keys or A and D to move. Press Space to fire, P to pause, and Return to start or restart.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. The game plays offline and does not require an account. Opening this support page or emailing support requires a connection.

Where is my high score?

Your high score is stored on the device where you played. It does not sync between your iPhone and Mac.

Why did the game pause?

The game pauses when you switch away from it. Use Resume when you return.

Report a problem

You can open a public GitHub issue if you have a GitHub account. Please do not post private information there. Email is available for private support.
