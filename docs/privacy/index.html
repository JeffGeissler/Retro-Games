Retro Eight Ball · Privacy

Your questions stay with you.

Retro Eight Ball works offline. The app does not collect or transmit personal data, questions, or answers.

Effective September 9, 2026 · Developer: Jeff Geissler

Using the app

You think of a question and tap SHAKE. There is no question-entry field, account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. Answers are generated on your device. The iOS app keeps the current answer only for the current app session; it does not maintain an answer history or upload answers.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, Jeff Geissler receives your email address and whatever message or attachments you send. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot problems. It is not used for advertising or sold. Email is handled through Outlook and the email providers involved in delivery.

Support correspondence may be retained to handle your request and related follow-up. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact scale.with.jeff@outlook.com. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Visiting these pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies, or contact forms. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Website hosting is separate from the offline app. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

External links

Opening the privacy page, support page, or email link uses your browser or email app. Those services process information under their own policies. If you post a bug report on GitHub, its contents are public; use email for private support.

Changes

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change, with a revised effective date. The statements above describe the current version.
