# App icon generation

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. The generated square artwork was resized to an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG for the Xcode app icon asset catalog.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final iOS app icon for Retro Eight Ball. Generate a single square 1024x1024 opaque full-bleed image, no rounded outer corners. A beautifully polished glossy black fortune-telling eight ball fills 85 percent of the square, centered, viewed straight on with a slight top highlight. In its front is a circular deep midnight-blue liquid window with one luminous inverted blue triangular die face floating inside. The triangle bears a single crisp white numeral '8', large and readable. Premium sculptural 3D rendering, simple bold silhouette readable at tiny app-icon size, restrained electric cyan rim lighting, rich cobalt blue triangle, near-black navy background filling every corner, subtle soft reflection on the black spherical shell. Match a retro arcade app but polished and minimal, not pixel art. Keep all main details in the central 80 percent for the system icon mask. No words, no extra numbers, no stars, no sparkles, no border around the square, no device mockup, no transparency, no watermark. Output only the icon artwork.
